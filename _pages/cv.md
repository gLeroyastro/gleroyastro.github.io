**Gavin Leroy, PhD**  
Postdoctoral Researcher in Astrophysics and Cosmology  
Durham University · Institute for Computational Cosmology (ICC) / Centre for Extragalactic Astronomy (CfEA)

This public CV summarises selected academic experience. For more details, see my [Durham University profile](https://dur.ac.uk/staff/gavin-leroy/) and [ORCID record](https://orcid.org/0009-0004-2523-4425).

## Appointments and education

- **May 2024–present:** Postdoctoral Researcher in Astrophysics, Durham University (ICC/CfEA), working with Prof. Richard Massey.
- **2020–2023:** PhD in Astrophysics and Astronomy, Université Paris-Saclay / CEA Saclay. Supervisors: Gabriel W. Pratt and Sandrine Pires. Thesis: *Euclid: Building the catalogue of galaxy clusters detected through weak gravitational lensing*.
- **2017–2020:** Engineering degree (Master's level) in Physics and Signal Processing, IMT Atlantique; specialisations in machine learning, artificial intelligence, signal processing and computer vision.
- **2019:** Academic semester in Astrophysics and Data Science, NTNU, Trondheim.
- **2019–2020:** Optical research assistant, Cristalens, Lannion.
- **2018:** Research assistant, Computational Electromagnetics Research Laboratory, Brest.

## Selected research leadership

- **Since 2025:** Co-lead of Euclid Key Project KP-DR1-CL-8 on self-interacting dark matter in galaxy clusters, coordinating **four working groups (about 30 researchers)** and running its regular meetings; lead of its paper on merging clusters.
- **Since 2024:** Lead of Euclid Key Paper KP-DR1-CL7-1, the DR1 weak-lensing galaxy-cluster catalogue, with a team of **about 15 researchers**.
- Co-lead, with A. Manjón-García, of a three-year Euclid challenge comparing **ten** weak-lensing cluster-detection algorithms (paper submitted).
- **Since 2024:** Member of the Euclid VIS operational unit (OU-VIS): Charge Transfer Inefficiency correction, its time-evolution pipeline, calibration and quality control.
- **Since 2024:** COSMOS-Web galaxy-shape calibration and dark-matter mass-map reconstruction; SuperBIT and SLICE collaboration work on gravitational lensing and mass mapping.
