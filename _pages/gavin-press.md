    <h2 id="press-feature-title">Mapping the invisible Universe</h2>
    <p>In January 2026, our international COSMOS-Web team published an ultra-high-resolution map of the matter distribution using weak gravitational lensing in James Webb Space Telescope observations. I was an equal-contribution author of the <em>Nature Astronomy</em> paper and helped reconstruct the mass map and identify galaxy groups and clusters.</p>
