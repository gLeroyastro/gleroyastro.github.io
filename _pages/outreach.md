I enjoy making astronomy accessible beyond the research community, from conversations about dark matter to interactive activities for schools and the public.

## Public engagement

- **2026 — Royal Society Summer Science Exhibition, London:** Selected, with a Royal Astronomical Society grant, to help create and present a Euclid exhibit, including 3D-printed models of stars, galaxies and dark-matter profiles.
- **Since 2024 — Durham University travelling planetarium:** Regular sessions with an inflatable planetarium in primary schools across the Durham region.
- **2024 and 2025 — Durham science festival:** Interactive experiments on gravitational lensing and how universes form, and immersive planetarium sessions for young children about the night sky and light pollution.
- **Since 2024 — Euclid UK outreach team:** Working with UK planetariums and museums, and closely with the French Euclid team, to bring Euclid images and data to the public.
- **2025 — Euclid Q1 data release:** Helped Durham University explain the first Euclid Quick Data Release to the public, in a [press release](https://www.durham.ac.uk/news-events/latest-news/2025/03/euclid-q1-data-release/) picked up by science media.
- **2025–2026 — Dark-matter cartography in the media:** Discussed the JWST dark-matter map with *National Geographic*, AP News, BBC Radio 4 and others; see [Press & media](/press/).
- **2025 — National Astronomy Meeting, Durham:** Chaired the organising committee of the *3 Minutes to Inspire* public-speaking competition: 12 PhD students, one-to-one mentoring and rehearsals, and an event for about 80 people.
- **2025 — Watercolours:** Painted three works for the public showing the Moon, a starry night and the northern lights.
- **2022 — Informal science evenings, Paris:** Two public events in *guinguettes scientifiques*, where researchers talk with the public in a relaxed setting.
- **2021 — Savanturiers science mentor:** Guided three classes of 15–16-year-olds through a research project on "Why is the night sky dark?", designing the question with their teachers and following the students' work.
- **2021 and 2020 — Science-communication competitions:** Second prize in *Ma thèse en 5 minutes* (*Ciel & Espace*), presenting my PhD to secondary-school students; joint second prize in *Mon stage en 3 minutes* (Fondation Mines-Télécom), representing IMT Atlantique.
- **2018–2020 — IMT Atlantique astronomy club:** President, then vice-president: astronomy talks and observing nights for students and families.

During my PhD I also earned a certificate in science communication and outreach (*Label en médiation et communication scientifique*).

## Community and scientific service

I contribute to research culture through event organisation, early-career support and committee work. This includes the Euclid early-career committee, Durham's machine-learning committee, and **Scraps of Science**, a seminar series on research skills that I founded.
