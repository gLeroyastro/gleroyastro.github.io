I enjoy making astronomy accessible beyond the research community, from conversations about dark matter to interactive activities for schools and the public.

## Public engagement

- **2026 — Royal Society Summer Science Exhibition, London:** Helped create and present a Euclid exhibit, including 3D-printed models of stars, galaxies and dark-matter profiles.
- **2026 — School planetarium programmes:** Presented mobile planetarium sessions in schools across North East England.
- **2025 — Dark-matter cartography in the media:** Discussed the JWST dark-matter map with science and news media, including interviews noted in my CV with *National Geographic*, AP News and BBC Radio 4.
- **2025 — National Astronomy Meeting, Durham:** Organised and chaired the public-speaking competition *3 Minutes to Inspire*, and supported science-outreach activities.
- **Since 2024 — Euclid public engagement:** Worked with planetariums and museums to showcase Euclid images and research; participated in science festivals and interactive demonstrations.

## Community and scientific service

I contribute to science and research culture through scientific-event organisation and early-career support. This includes the Euclid Early-Career Researchers Committee, machine-learning activities at Durham, and **Scraps of Science**, a seminar series dedicated to professional skills in research.

I have also helped organise events including the National Astronomy Meeting, ASTRODAT, SLICE meetings and Euclid activities.
