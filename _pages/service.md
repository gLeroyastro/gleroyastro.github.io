Leading collaborative science and supporting the research community are a large part of my work.

## Scientific leadership

- **Since 2025 — Co-lead, Euclid Key Project KP-DR1-CL-8 (self-interacting dark matter with galaxy clusters).** I coordinate and structure the work of four Euclid working groups (about 30 researchers), run the project's regular meetings and steer its preparation for the first year of Euclid data. I also lead its DR1 paper on merging clusters.
- **Since 2024 — Lead, Euclid Key Paper KP-DR1-CL7-1 (weak-lensing galaxy-cluster catalogue).** I steer how the selected detection algorithms are applied to DR1 data, in a team of about 15 researchers, working closely with the groups on mass measurement, optical detection and cluster morphology.
- **Euclid cluster-detection challenge.** I co-led, with Alberto Manjón-García, a three-year comparison of ten weak-lensing detection algorithms within a Euclid working group, to select the methods used on real Euclid data (paper submitted).
- **Since 2024 — Euclid VIS operational unit (OU-VIS).** Charge-transfer-inefficiency correction, its time-evolution pipeline, and quality control of VIS images.
- **Collaborations:** Euclid Consortium (since 2020), COSMOS-Web, SuperBIT and SLICE (since 2024).

## Conference and meeting organisation

- **National Astronomy Meeting 2025, Durham:** local organising committee (outreach activities; chair of *3 Minutes to Inspire*), and scientific organising committee of the session *The Era of Gravitational Lensing*, including reviewing and selecting contributions.
- **DEX, Edinburgh:** local and scientific organising committees for this two-day meeting of universities in the north of England; selecting contributions and supporting communication and logistics.
- **Euclid Consortium 2026 welcome session (online):** organising and scientific committee for the session welcoming new consortium members.
- **ASTRODAT (first edition), Durham:** local organising committee for a week-long workshop on statistics in astrophysics and cosmology; website and communication.
- **SLICE annual meeting:** local organising committee.
- **Euclid UK CTI meeting:** local organising committee for the national meeting on charge-transfer inefficiency and its correction.
- **Elbereth conference, IAP, Paris:** organising committee; designed the poster and other communication material.

## Committees and community

- **Machine-learning committee, Durham Astrophysics:** postdoc representative. I organise seminars on machine learning in astronomy, and created and run a regular 4-hour training workshop for astronomy PhD students.
- **Euclid early-career researchers committee:** mentoring sessions, a library of resources on getting permanent positions in the consortium's member countries, and help with difficult professional situations.
- **Scraps of Science, Durham:** founder and postdoc representative of this seminar series on research skills (scientific writing, networking, public speaking), mixing short talks with discussion of personal experience.
- **Social committee, Durham Astrophysics:** social events, welcoming new PhD students and postdocs, and promoting equality, diversity and inclusion.
- **St John's College, Durham (since 2024):** invited non-permanent member of the governing body, contributing to discussions of the college's academic and social direction and to choosing its seminars and activities.
- **2020–2023 — Galaxy-cluster and weak-lensing journal club, CEA Paris-Saclay:** lead; choosing papers, coordinating speakers and leading discussion between PhD students, postdocs and permanent staff.

## Volunteering

- **IMT Atlantique alumni association:** vice-president and representative of PhD graduates; member of the board. I work with the school's leadership to promote its PhD students and graduates, and organise mentoring and events with PhD alumni.
- **Cake for Goods, Durham University:** volunteer baker for monthly charity sales supporting local causes, including women in science and disadvantaged children.
