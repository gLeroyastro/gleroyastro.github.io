I teach computational and experimental methods in physics and astrophysics, and contribute to research supervision and skills development.

## Durham University

**Autumn 2025 — Master's level (M2)**

- **Machine learning applied to physics:** 16 h of practical classes; preparing and marking reports (about 16 h) for about 60 students.
- **Scientific computing applied to physics:** 16 h of practical classes and 2 h of lectures (as a substitute lecturer); marking reports (about 16 h) for about 60 students.

**Autumn 2024 — Master's level (M2)**

- **High-performance computing (HPC):** 16 h of practical classes; preparing and marking projects (about 16 h) for about 70 students; two weeks of C-programming refresher workshops for international students (about 12 h).
- **Scientific computing applied to physics:** 16 h of practical classes; preparing and marking projects (about 16 h) for about 70 students.

## IUT d'Orsay, Université Paris-Saclay

- **Spring 2022:** Optics and electronics, second-year undergraduates: 32 h of practical classes, plus preparation and the oral jury for 16 students' final projects.
- **Autumn 2021:** Metrology and electronics, first-year undergraduates: 32 h of practical classes for 19 students, including designing the experimental protocols and marking lab reports.

## Supervision and mentoring

- **Since 2024:** Co-supervisor of a Durham University PhD project on pixel-bounce calibration for Euclid VIS charge-transfer inefficiency correction.
- **Since 2024:** Admissions examiner and interview coach at IMT Atlantique: admission and assessment juries for second- and third-year engineering students (French and international), and mock interviews with personalised feedback.
- **2022:** Co-supervised an 8-week internship by a second-year engineering student at CEA Paris-Saclay on convolutional neural networks for galaxy-cluster detection in convergence maps, from implementation to performance analysis.
- **At Durham:** I created and run a regular 4-hour machine-learning workshop for astronomy PhD students, and founded *Scraps of Science*, seminars on research skills such as scientific writing, networking and public speaking. See [Service & leadership](/service/).
