**My contribution (first author):** this work

1. implements the main weak-lensing galaxy-cluster detection algorithms from the literature;
2. develops a new multiscale (wavelet-based) detection method optimised for the properties of galaxy clusters;
3. compares the performance of all methods on simulated data.
