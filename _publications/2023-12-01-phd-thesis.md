Supervised by Gabriel W. Pratt and Sandrine Pires at CEA Saclay.

The thesis develops and implements a **complete methodology for detecting galaxy clusters from their total mass distribution**, including dark matter, through weak gravitational lensing. The method is applied to simulated data and to real data from the Hyper Suprime-Cam Subaru Strategic Program (HSC-SSP), and the associated biases are characterised quantitatively.
