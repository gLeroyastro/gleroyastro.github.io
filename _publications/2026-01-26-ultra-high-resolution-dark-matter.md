**My contribution (equal-contribution second author):**

- Reconstructed the projected mass map from JWST COSMOS-Web weak-lensing data with the improved **KS+** method; this map is the paper's main figure.
- Applied my multiscale detection algorithm to the reconstructed map and produced the **first catalogue of galaxy groups and clusters** detected in it.
- Validated this catalogue against X-ray and optical data from the COSMOS-Web collaboration.

The result was covered by *National Geographic*, AP, *BBC Sky at Night* and others; see [Press & media](/press/).
