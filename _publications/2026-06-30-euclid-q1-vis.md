**My contribution:** as a member of the Euclid VIS operational unit (OU-VIS), I contributed to the processing of Euclid's VIS images. I carried out the validation tests of the **charge-transfer inefficiency (CTI) correction** and the control of related systematic effects on the Q1 data, using the EPER method described in Section 3.8.
