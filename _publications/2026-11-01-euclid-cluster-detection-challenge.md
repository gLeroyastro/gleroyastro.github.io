**My contribution (equal-contribution second author):** this paper selects the weak-lensing galaxy-cluster detection algorithms that will be applied to real Euclid data. Together with Alberto Manjón-García, I co-led the comparative analysis of **ten detection methods** in a **three-year challenge** run within a Euclid Consortium working group.
